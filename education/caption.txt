How does RF microneedling actually work? 🔬✨

Ultra-fine microneedles create tiny channels in the skin, and radiofrequency energy delivers gentle heat deep into the dermis. Your skin responds by producing fresh collagen and elastin — for firmer, smoother, more even-looking skin.

Results build gradually over 8–12 weeks and are best with a series of 3–4 sessions.
