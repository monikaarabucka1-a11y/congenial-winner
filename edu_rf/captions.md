## Caption (EN)

How does RF microneedling actually work? 🤔✨

1️⃣ Micro-channels — ultra-fine needles create tiny, controlled micro-injuries in the skin. Your body reads them as a signal to repair.
2️⃣ RF energy — through the needles, radio-frequency heat is delivered into the deeper layers of the skin (the dermis), exactly where collagen is made.
3️⃣ New collagen — over the next 4–6 weeks your skin rebuilds fresh collagen and elastin, so it looks firmer, smoother and more refined.

Why combine the two? Needles alone work on the surface; RF on its own heats without channels. Together they reach deeper, with less downtime.

✔️ Best for: fine lines, enlarged pores, acne scars, loss of firmness
⏳ Downtime: light redness for 1–2 days
📈 Results: build gradually, best after a course of 3 sessions
